The nightly card run

Every night the project's FPGA card runs one resumed stress run: a battery of checks, then slices of exhaustive sweeps in which the library's answers are compared, bit for bit, with an oracle on the host's CPU. This page shows how far those sweeps have come. It is rebuilt after each night from the run's own record by hw/card_stress_progress.py, and holds no figure of its own.
