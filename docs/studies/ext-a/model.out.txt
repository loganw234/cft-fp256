  for scale: one desktop core under MPFR = 1.5e+13 bp/s; at ~7.5 mm2 a core, ~1.9e+12 bp/s/mm2
  binary4096, a 16-stage pipe held on `en`: ~259,904 flops (~1.6M gates) beside one p x 24 column (~585k gates)
