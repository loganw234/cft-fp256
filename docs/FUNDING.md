# Funding landscape

Tracked options for funding the open deterministic-arithmetic program,
ordered by sequence rather than size. What this project brings that
most applicants lack: a verified artifact (golden model + RTL green in
CI), the atlas one-hash-across-three-GPU-vendors demonstration, an
enforced open-toolchain portability gate, and a conformance ladder
with real prices ($150 attestation node -> FPGA tiles -> shuttle
silicon).

## Now (solo-dev scale)

| funder | scale | fit | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| NLnet / NGI Zero | EUR 5-50k | exact - they fund the Yosys/nextpnr/open-silicon genre | rolling calls ~every 2 months; low bureaucracy; apply first |
| IHP open-PDK shuttles | tapeout in kind | the chiplet fabrication path | apply when chiplet RTL exists; BMBF-funded free shuttles for accepted open designs |

## At ecosystem scale (needs partners / evidence of dependence)

| funder | scale | fit | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sovereign Tech Agency | EUR 100k-1M | critical open infrastructure | funded the Yosys ecosystem; wants downstream dependence |
| NSF POSE | $300k / $1.5M phases | open-source ecosystem building | needs US institutional partner |
| SBIR/STTR (NSF/DoD/DOE) | $250k-1M+ | verifiable computation hardware | requires incorporating; non-dilutive |
| Horizon Europe / Chips JU | EUR 1M+ | open EDA / RISC-V adjacent calls | consortium bureaucracy; EU partners required |

## Partnership multipliers (not grants, force multipliers)

- University groups in reconfigurable computing / numerical analysis
  (Paderborn's FPGA cluster offers external U280 time; ETH/PULP;
  Imperial) - unlock national grants, students, and hardware.
- CHIPS Alliance / OpenHW Foundation - consortium stewardship for a
  neutral IEEE-754 determinism conformance suite; governance and
  member engineering rather than cash.
- lowRISC - the OpenTitan open-design/commercial-fab precedent;
  engineering-house partnership model.
